# Code of Conduct

We welcome participation regardless of experience, identity or background.
Be respectful, use constructive criticism and focus technical disagreement on
ideas and evidence. Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexualized
attention, publication of private information and sustained disruption are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing this project.

Report conduct concerns privately to **alvit.work@gmail.com**. Include the
relevant links, dates and requested confidentiality precautions. Do not post
private reports in a public issue. The primary maintainer reviews reports,
limits disclosure to what is necessary for a fair response, and may request a
correction, remove content, limit participation or exclude an offender according
to severity and repetition. Participants may request reconsideration with new
information through the same contact.

This project has one maintainer and no independent moderator or escalation
contact. A complaint involving that maintainer therefore has no independent
project adjudicator; GitHub's own abuse-reporting process remains available.
Confidentiality cannot be guaranteed against legal requirements or platform access.
For software vulnerabilities use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
